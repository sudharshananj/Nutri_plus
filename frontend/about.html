About Nutrition Plus & System Architecture

Nutrition Plus is an academic mini-project for the Computer Science & Engineering department, developed to demonstrate transparent, explainable, rule-based recommendation systems for adult general wellness without relying on external paid APIs or black-box algorithms.

Rule-Based Engine Architecture

1. Input Validation Layer (validator.py) Verifies age ≥ 18, allow-lists enums, sanitizes strings, strictly rejects medical history or diagnosis keys.
Stage 1
↓
2. Safety Exclusion Filter (safety_filter.py) Runs FIRST. Eliminates every meal containing declared allergens or violating eating pattern. Scans ingredient text for keyword aliases (paneer, curd, atta, groundnut, etc.). Never overridden.
Safety Non-Negotiable
↓
3. Deterministic Weighted Ranker (ranker.py) Calculates deterministic 0–10 score (+3.0 cuisine match, +2.0 scaled prep time, +2.0 budget match, +2.0 goal match, +1.0 variety bonus, + stored feedback weights).
Stage 3
↓
4. Movement Selector & Explainer (workout_selector.py, explainer.py) Filters workouts by equipment and duration, maps intensity to activity level, generates transparent rationale text for each recommendation.
Stage 4

Mandatory Safety & Boundary Rules

  • Adults 18+ Only: Strictly verified at validation level. Anyone under 18 receives an explicit refusal.
  • Zero Medical Information: We do not collect medical history, medical conditions, medications, or lab values. Inputting medical fields triggers immediate rejection.
  • Allergy Exclusions Run Before Ranking: User safety takes precedence over all preferences. Safety filters cannot be relaxed by feedback scoring.
  • Soft Preference Learning: Feedback (like, dislike, skip, swap) only modifies soft preference dimensions (cuisine, cost tier, workout category) clamped to [-3, +3].
  • Unverified Sample Nutrients: Calorie and macronutrient estimates are sample home-cooking baseline values for general awareness, not lab-verified clinical measurements.

Scientific & Security References

  1. World Health Organization (WHO): Healthy Diet Fact Sheet (2026 update). Emphasizes diversified whole foods, dietary fiber, vegetables, and limiting free sugars and saturated fats.
  2. World Health Organization (WHO): Guidelines on Physical Activity and Sedentary Behaviour (2020). Recommends 150–300 minutes of moderate-intensity activity weekly for adults.
  3. OWASP API Security Top 10 (2023): Implemented broken object level authorization defenses (BOLA), parameterised SQL queries, request size limiting, and strict allow-list input validation.
